VI. Learn Together

Make music with others.

Group music-making develops skills that private lessons alone can’t teach — timing, dynamics, and musical communication.

Featured program · Now enrolling

Youth Band

Our Youth Band brings together current SFLCM students to rehearse and perform as an ensemble. Students learn to listen, blend, follow a conductor, and experience the thrill of making music as a group.

Ages 9–14, current students
